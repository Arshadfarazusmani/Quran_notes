## 📌 1. Pattern Overview: If'aal (إِفْعَال)
This pattern often indicates "causing" an action or transitioning into a state. It is the root of common words like *Islam* and *Imaan*.

### **The "Master Key" Pattern: Islaam (إِسْلَام)**


| Form | Arabic Pattern | Sample Word (Arabic) | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masdar (Root)** | *If'aal* | **Islaam** (إِسْلَام) | To submit |
| **Past Active** | *Af'ala* | **Aslama** (أَسْلَمَ) | He submitted |
| **Past Passive** | *Uf'ila* | **Uslima** (أُسْلِمَ) | He was submitted |
| **Present Active** | *Yuf'ilu* | **Yuslimu** (يُسْلِمُ) | He submits |
| **Present Passive**| *Yuf'alu* | **Yuslamu** (يُسْلَمُ) | He is being submitted |
| **Doer (Subject)** | *Muf'il* | **Muslim** (مُسْلِم) | The Submitter (Muslim) |
| **Object (Maf'ool)**| *Muf'al* | **Muslam** (مُسْلَم) | One submitted to |

## 📌 3. Pattern Overview: Taf'eel (تَفْعِيل)
This pattern often signifies intensive, repeated, or transformative action.

### **The "Master Key" Pattern: Tasbeeh (تَسْبِيح)**


| Form | Arabic Pattern | Sample Word (Arabic) | Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masdar (Root)** | *Taf'eel* | **Tasbeeh** (تَسْبِيح) | To glorify |
| **Past Active** | *Fa''ala* | **Sabbaha** (سَبَّحَ) | He glorified |
| **Past Passive** | *Fu''ila* | **Subbiha** (سُبِّحَ) | He was glorified |
| **Present Active** | *Yufabbbi'u*| **Yusabbihu** (يُسَبِّحُ) | He glorifies |
| **Present Passive**| *Yufabba'u* | **Yusabbahu** (يُسَبَّحُ) | He is being glorified |
| **Doer (Subject)** | *Mufa''il* | **Musabbih** (مُسَبِّح) | One who glorifies |
| **Object (Maf'ool)**| *Mufa''al* | **Musabbah** (مُسَبَّح) | The glorified one |

## 💡 5. Crucial Summary Tips
1. **The "Mu-" Start:** All Doers (Subject) and Objects in these complex patterns begin with a **Mu-** (مُ).
2. **Vowel Change:** * **Zer ( ِ )** on the 2nd-to-last letter = **The Doer** (e.g., *Mufassir*).
    * **Zabar ( َ )** on the 2nd-to-last letter = **The Object** (e.g., *Muharram*).
3. **Plurals:** * Masculine: Add **-oona** (ـُون) or **-eena** (ـِين) (e.g., *Muslimeen*, *Mu'minoon*).
    * Feminine: Add **-aat** (ـَات) (e.g., *Muslimaat*, *Mu'minaat*).
